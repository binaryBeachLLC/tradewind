1 · Summary

The product. A sealed, solar-powered sensor that reports air temperature and humidity, soil moisture and rainfall over LoRaWAN every 5 minutes (1 minute in rapid mode), for farms and remote sites. No buttons, no battery changes: a magnet tap on the lid wakes it, and the panel keeps a protected 18650 cell topped up.

The hard part was heat, not electrons. Li-ion cells must not charge above 45–50 °C, and a box in the Hawaiian sun gets far hotter than the air. Simulation showed a grey box reaching 51 °C and a panel glued to the lid 55 °C (5.4 h a day with charging blocked). Rev B mounts the panel on a ventilated roof 25 mm above a white ASA box: 35 °C in simulation, 37.1 °C measured in September.

What changed from rev A. Bring-up found two bugs: sleep current of 34 µA (the battery-sense divider leaked into the ADC clamp diode) and 2–6 rain counts per bucket tip (reed bounce). Both were reproduced in simulation, fixed in rev B and verified. The antenna match was tuned with the lid closed, which moved return loss at 915 MHz from -11.1 to -32.7 dB.

Schematic notes, block by block

BlockDecision and the reason for it
Solar input
J1, D1, Q1, C1, C2		D1 SMAJ12CA (bidirectional, 12 V standoff, 19.9 V clamp at 20 A) takes surges and ESD on the panel lead. Q1 DMP3099L is a P-FET in the high side with its gate pulled to GND (R1): a correct panel turns it on, a reversed panel leaves it off. It is rated −30 V with ±20 V on the gate, above both the clamp voltage and the panel's 6.0 V open circuit. A FET drops about 45 mV at the input limit; a Schottky would drop about 0.35 V, which the 4.5 V input-regulation point can't spare. C1 10 µF / 25 V keeps about half its value at 5 V of bias, inside the charger's 1–10 µF range for IN.
Charger
U2 BQ24074		USB500 mode (EN1 = VSYS, EN2 = GND): the input is limited to 475 mA and VIN-DPM holds the panel at 4.5 V, which is the reason this part works without an MPPT chip. EN1 is tied to VSYS, not to the panel, because the EN pins are rated 7 V. ISET 1.78 kΩ sets 500 mA. TMR is grounded so the safety timers can't end a slow solar charge. The TS NTC limits charging to about 0–50 °C. ILIM is fitted as the datasheet asks (USB500 ignores it). CHG and PGOOD pull up to +3V3, not VSYS, so the MCU pins never exceed VDD.

Schematic notes (continued)

BlockDecision and the reason for it
Charger heatWorst-case linear-charger dissipation is (VIN − VBAT) × I ≈ (5.0 − 3.2 V) × 0.40 A ≈ 0.7 W, at a low cell with a strong panel. At 44.5 °C/W junction to air (datasheet, JEDEC board) that is a 31 °C rise, so about 91 °C in a 60 °C box. The charger's own thermal regulation (125 °C) cuts the current before it gets dangerous. U2 sits 76 mm from the internal humidity sensor, and the measured coupling is 3.1 °C per watt.
Buck-boost
U3 TPS63900		The cell crosses 3.3 V during discharge, so the converter must both step up and step down. Its quiescent current is 75 nA. The output is set by CFG resistors read once at power-up (R8 16.2 kΩ → 3.3 V), so they are 1 %, 100 ppm parts on short traces: a misread sets the wrong rail. The pins tolerate 10 pF; these traces add 0.2 pF. With the input-current limit set to unlimited, the converter's peak switch current is 1.33–1.6 A and TI asks for an inductor with Isat ≥ 2 A. L1 is a 2.2 µH molded metal-alloy part rated 2.6 A. C7 and C8 are 22 µF / 10 V X5R and keep about 60 % at 3.3 V, and the transmit-droop simulation (3.4) uses that derated value.
Battery sense
Q2, Q3, R11, R12, C10		The rev A low-side switch leaked 27–40 µA through the ADC clamp. Rev B uses a high-side switch, which leaks 3 nA when off. After Q2 turns on, C10 charges through R11 ‖ R12 (50 kΩ), with τ = 0.5 ms. A 12-bit reading needs 4.5 ms to settle to ½ LSB. Found in this review: the firmware waited 3 ms, which reads 0.25 % low (≈ 10 mV at 4 V). It now waits 5 ms.
Radio / MCU
U1 RAK3172		The module carries the FCC modular approval, the STM32WLE5 and its 32 MHz reference, all behind a shield can. VDD gets 10 µF + 100 nF at the pin. BOOT0 is pulled down, and NRST has 100 nF plus a reset switch inside the case. Debug: a Tag-Connect footprint (no connector to buy) and UART pads for AT commands and the bootloader.
RF match
C14, L2, C15		The π footprint carries a shunt C (C14 1.8 pF C0G ±0.1 pF), a series L (L2 7.5 nH, high-Q) and a spare shunt (C15, DNP). Values come from S11 measured in the closed case and the optimizer in sim/rf_match.py. Shunt-C plus series-L is a low-pass, so it also cuts the harmonics (4.6).
Air probe
J4, F1, R19, R20		The probe stays powered (80 nA idle): an unpowered SHT45 would clamp the shared I²C bus through its ESD diodes. F1 (100 mA PTC) protects +3V3 from a crushed cable. R19/R20 (100 Ω) limit ESD current into the MCU and damp cable ringing; the 4.7 kΩ pull-ups sit on the board side.
Soil probe
U7, R21, R22, C21		TPS22917 load switch: the probe is powered only for its 100 ms warm-up. CT 1 nF slows turn-on so the probe's input capacitance doesn't pull +3V3 down. R21 (1 MΩ) reads a missing probe as 0 V. R22 and C21 (1.6 kHz low-pass) remove the probe's own oscillator ripple.
Rain / magnet
R23, R24, C22, U4		The 1 MΩ pull-up draws 3.3 µA only while the reed is closed. The 10 kΩ / 22 nF filter rejects bounce (τfall 0.22 ms, τrise 22 ms) and bypasses RF picked up on the gauge lead. The DRV5032FB is push-pull, so it needs no pull-up current.

4.2 Electrical design considerations

The board is small and slow by most standards; the hard limits are microamps in sleep, a 0.5 A linear charger, a +20 dBm transmitter and four cables that leave the box. Every number below is measured off the routed board by tools/layout_audit.py and evaluated in sim/board_si.py.

1 oz outer copper; ΔT from the IPC-2221 external-layer curve. A 0.3 mm track carries 1.0 A for a 10 °C rise, so heating is never the issue; resistance is. The drops that matter: the 31 mV on VBAT during constant-current charge makes the charger enter constant-voltage about 31 mV early, which lengthens the charge slightly but costs no capacity (the drop falls to ~3 mV at the 50 mA termination current). VIN_SOLAR's 25 mV is harmless: VIN-DPM regulates at the IN pin, so the panel simply runs 25 mV higher on its curve. Rev C widens VBAT to 1 mm (9 mV).

Plane pair and decoupling

L2 (GND) and L3 (+3V3) are 1.07 mm apart, which gives only 242 pF of plane capacitance. The design doesn't rely on it. Decoupling is by capacitors at each supply pin: 10 µF + 100 nF at the module, 100 nF at each sensor and the hall switch, and 22 µF × 2 at the converter. The module's TX step (5 → 87 mA in ~10 µs) is carried by C12 and the converter output. Simulated droop is 32.6 mV; measured 40 mV.

I²C over the probe cable

The internal bus (SHT40, 87 mm of trace at 0.89 pF/cm) is 23 pF and rises in 91 ns. The 1 m probe cable adds about 100 pF per conductor. The firmware therefore clocks at 100 kHz, which tolerates 2.1 m with the fitted 4.7 kΩ pull-ups. 400 kHz would need 2.2 kΩ and a cable under 1.2 m. That would gain nothing, because the bus moves 12 bytes per reading. SDA and SCL run side by side in the unshielded cable, so crosstalk is limited by keeping the edges slow: 4.7 kΩ pull-ups and 100 Ω series resistors.

Assumed: 5 pF/MCU pin, 10 pF/SHT4x, 0.25 pF ESD, cable 100 pF/m. Measure the cable before raising the clock.

JLC 4-layer standard: 0.15 mm track and space, 0.2 mm drill, 0.3 mm to edge. DRC with schematic parity: 0 errors. L1 sits 0.21 mm over the ground plane, so every top-layer trace has a tight, unbroken return path directly beneath it. That is the main reason for choosing 4 layers on a board this slow.

Layer usage and return paths

  • L2 is unbroken GND under the whole board; only via barrels perforate it. Every L1 trace returns directly beneath itself.
  • L4 traces reference the +3V3 plane (L3), not ground. That return path closes through decoupling capacitors, so only slow nets are allowed on L4. The 14 nets with copper there are DC or kHz signals: +3V3_EXT, AUX_ADC, CFG2, CHG_N, HALL_N, ILIM, PGOOD_N, RAIN_IN, SOIL_ADC, SOLAR_SENSE, TS, UART2_RX, UART2_TX, VBAT. RF, the switch node and I²C stay on L1.
  • Plane connections: every GND and +3V3 pad has its own via (72 GND, 21 +3V3), so no part shares a via with a neighbour's return current. U2's pad has 4 thermal vias and U3's has 6, both to L2.

Placement and zoning

  • One cable wall. J1–J5 line the bottom edge, so every cable leaves through the box's bottom wall (four glands and the SMA bulkhead). Water runs off the glands, and cable currents stay along one edge, far from the RF line.
  • Noisy and quiet zones. Power (charger and buck-boost) sits bottom-left beside J1, field I/O bottom-middle, and RF bottom-right next to J5. The switch node is 36 mm from the RF line, 40 mm from the U.FL and 62 mm from the internal humidity sensor.
  • Thermal. The two heat sources (charger U2, module U1 during transmit) are 76 mm and 18 mm from U5. U5 also sits on a peninsula cut by an L-shaped milled slot, with no copper pour on any layer under it.
  • Cell band. The 18650 holder takes the top 33 mm. The NTC (RT1) sits against the cell body, so the charger sees the cell's temperature, not the board's.

6 · Firmware

RAKwireless RUI3 (Arduino-style API) on the module's STM32WLE5. LoRaWAN 1.0.3 OTAA, US915 sub-band 2, ADR on. The payload packer and the network-server decoder share golden test vectors: 16 C++ and 22 JavaScript host tests pass.

Per-uplink sequence

  1. RTC alarm wakes from STOP2 (7.2 µA board total).
  2. SENSOR_EN on, then wait 100 ms for the soil probe. Read both SHT4x (CRC-checked).
  3. VBAT switch on, wait 5 ms, read the ADC. Also read panel voltage, CHG and PGOOD.
  4. Pack 11 bytes, then send unconfirmed on fPort 1 at SF9 (206 ms at +20 dBm).
  5. RX1/RX2 windows, then apply any downlink (interval, rapid mode, health, reboot).
  6. Sleep until the next deadline. Rain tips and magnet taps wake it early.

Design for the field

  • Water. All cables leave downward through glands, with drip loops; the antenna points down; the vent sits on a side wall below the gasket line.
  • Heat. White ASA (solar absorptance ≈ 0.25) under a roof that blocks direct sun on the top and sides. Measured in the field: interior 3.8 °C above air on average, 5.4 °C worst.
  • UV. ASA coupons changed ΔE 1.7 after 500 h of UV-A, against 8.9 for PETG, which is why PETG was rejected.
  • Production. The walls are drafted and evenly thick so the printed shapes carry straight into injection-molding tooling. The roof and shield plates are flat-pack.

11 · Conclusions and next steps

Rev B is ready for a pilot build. It meets every electrical, radio and sensing requirement with margin. Charging is marginal only at the edges of the NTC window. The enclosure's ingress rating needs one accredited IP66 jet test before it goes on a datasheet as certified.
